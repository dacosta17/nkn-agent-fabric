# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, and keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and engineering trade-offs.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. For safety-sensitive concerns, use the private reporting guidance in `SECURITY.md`.
